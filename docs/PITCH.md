# Pause: pitch, demo video and X post

## One line

The AI agent that plans, secures and pays for your group.

## 30-second pitch (say it out loud)

Every group that raises money, for a reunion, a wedding, a trip or an ajo, has one tired person chasing payments, and one fear: what if the money disappears?

Pause joins your group chat. It plans the budget, collects everyone's share into one pot, and pays only the vendors the group approves, after a short window where anyone can object. If someone slips in a scammer, Pause refuses to pay.

The rules live in a contract on NEAR, not in the AI. Pause's own key can only propose a payment and pay what members approved. NEAR refuses anything else. The planning runs on NEAR AI Cloud's private models.

It's live on the web and in Telegram today.
